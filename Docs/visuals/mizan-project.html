A student budgeting system
built from first principles.

Mizān solves a precise problem: Israeli university students run out of money because their bank balance includes committed obligations. The app separates what's genuinely free to spend from what's already spoken for — and tells you one honest number every day.

Why this exists

Bank balance is a lie.

The balance in your account includes money earmarked for rent, tuition, and phone bills. Nothing deducts these obligations until they actually clear — creating a false sense of available funds and recurring end-of-month shortfalls.

"Nothing deducts future charges from the balance until they actually clear. A student sees ₪2,000 available and spends freely — then rent hits and they're in crisis."
Concept.md · Problem Statement

Committed obligations are invisible

Rent due next week still shows as "available" in the bank. The student has no systemic way to deduct it until it clears.

Irregular income makes it harder

Family transfers, scholarships, and part-time work arrive unpredictably. Simple monthly budgets break when income isn't uniform.

No visibility into spend trajectory

Students have no daily signal for whether they're on pace. They discover the problem at month-end, when it's too late to adjust.

The core mechanic

Free Money — the only
number that matters.

The BalanceEngine computes Free Money deterministically from a session opening balance, all income entries, all committed charges, and all logged transactions. No estimates, no bank sync required.

BalanceEngine · free_money formula
+ opening_balance ₪2,000
+ total_income ₪835
− total_committed ₪1,200
− total_spent ₪995
= free_money ₪640
on_track_pct · monthly signal
monthly_spent ÷ monthly_budget × 100 = 35%
Core invariant

Committed charges deduct at log time — not due date

The moment you log a committed charge, it is immediately subtracted from Free Money. Even if rent isn't due for two weeks, it's already gone from your usable balance. This is the mechanic that makes the system honest.

Session model

One continuous session — no forced monthly reset

The AppSession persists across months with an opening_balance. This handles irregular income and charges that don't align with calendar months.

Fuzzy charges

Uncertain charges never touch Free Money directly

A fuzzy charge (course books, ₪80–150) is tracked separately and shown on the timeline. It only enters the formula when resolved to a confirmed amount — never as an estimate.

Caution threshold

Caution triggers at 7 × average daily spend

The CAUTION state fires when Free Money drops below 7 times the student's average daily spend — a student-overridable buffer designed to give enough runway to adjust.

State machines

Two independent state signals.
Always explicit, never silent.

The BalanceEngine computes two separate state values on every snapshot — BalanceState (based on Free Money) and OnTrackState (based on monthly pace) — which drive the dashboard's color signals independently.

BalanceState — based on Free Money
NORMAL

Safe to spend

Free Money is above the caution threshold. The green hero card. Daily number is reliable.

free_money > 7 × avg_daily
CAUTION

Buffer running low

Free Money is positive but within 7 days of average spend. Time to cut back before it becomes a crisis.

0 < free_money ≤ 7 × avg_daily
CRISIS

In the red

Free Money is at or below zero. Committed charges exceed available funds. The red hero card — cannot be hidden or softened.

free_money ≤ 0
OnTrackState — based on monthly spend pace
GREEN

On pace

Monthly spend is within budget. Safe daily is positive.

on_track_pct < 100%
YELLOW

Over budget

Monthly spend has exceeded the monthly budget but hasn't hit the crisis threshold yet.

100% ≤ pct < 130%
RED

Well over budget

Spend is 30%+ over the monthly budget. Significant adjustment required.

pct ≥ 130%
TIGHT_MONTH

No budget headroom

Monthly budget is zero or negative — income this month doesn't exceed committed charges.

monthly_budget ≤ 0

Architecture

Four strict layers.
One direction of dependency.

CLI → Services → Domain + Ports ← Infrastructure. No layer may reach across or skip a layer. Repository protocols make storage swappable with no domain change.

Design invariants

Rules that cannot be broken
at any stage.

These invariants are enforced at every layer and cannot be softened, routed around, or deferred to a later stage. They exist because violating any of them breaks the user's trust in the number.

INV-01

Free money never displayed as normal in crisis

The BalanceState drives the hero card colour. CRISIS always renders red — it cannot be overridden by the student, a setting, or a theme.

BalanceState.CRISIS → red hero, always
INV-02

Budget signal always from free money — never bank balance

The dashboard number is computed, not fetched. Bank balance is read once at session init and never again used as the display figure.

display = BalanceEngine.free_money()
INV-03

Committed charge deducts at log time, not due date

This is the core mechanic. The moment a charge is logged, it reduces free_money. Its due_date is only used for reminder surfacing.

total_committed = sum(all upcoming charges)
INV-04

Fuzzy charges never enter the free_money formula

A fuzzy charge is tracked and shown on the UI but never touches the formula until resolved. Guesses don't become facts until confirmed.

fuzzy → timeline only, not formula
INV-05

AppSession must exist before any financial write

Income, charges, and transactions are rejected with ApplicationError if no active session exists. Orphaned financial data cannot exist.

SessionService.get_active() gates all writes
INV-06

Insight fires at most once per category per period

The pattern detection engine (Stage 4) may surface one spend insight per category per month — never more. The system will not flood the student with observations. One relevant signal at the right moment is the design target, not a notification feed.

insight_count[category][period] ≤ 1
Charge lifecycles

Three types of charge.
Three distinct state machines.

Each charge type has its own lifecycle. Recurring charges auto-generate their next occurrence on mark-paid. Fuzzy charges only enter the formula on resolution — never before.

CommittedCharge

One-off

UPCOMING
Logged. Amount deducted from free_money immediately.
PAID
Marked paid by developer. Removed from upcoming charges. Terminal state.
CommittedCharge + RecurringRule

Recurring

UPCOMING
Auto-generated from RecurringRule. Deducts immediately.
PAID
Marked paid. Triggers auto-generation of next occurrence.
UPCOMING (next)
New charge created for day_of_month next month. Cycle continues.
FuzzyCharge

Uncertain

PENDING
Logged. Shown on timeline. Not in free_money formula.
OVERDUE (optional)
Past due_date without resolution. Surfaced in UI as warning.
RESOLVED
Confirmed amount becomes a CommittedCharge. Deducts for the first time.
DISCARDED
Charge won't happen. Removed from timeline. No impact on formula.
