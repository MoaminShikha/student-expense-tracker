The Problem
Students go broke by surprise.
💸
The Balance Illusion
Your bank shows ₪2,400. Looks safe. But rent (₪1,800), electricity (₪200), and a subscription (₪60) haven't cleared yet. Real spendable: ₪340.
"I had money, then suddenly I didn't."
📅
Invisible Upcoming Charges
Rent, tuition installments, utilities, subscriptions — they're all committed costs that don't show up in your balance until it's too late to act.
"I forgot the internet bill was coming out."
🤷
No Daily Budget Signal
Even students who track expenses have no idea how much they can spend today without compromising the rest of the month. Spreadsheets don't give you a number.
"I track my spending but I still overspend."
🌫️
Fuzzy Unknowns
Variable bills — electricity, groceries — have no fixed amount. You know they're coming but can't plan. Most apps ignore this; they only track what you've already spent.
"My electric bill changes every month."
The Solution
One honest number: Free Money.
Mizān borrows a concept from corporate treasury — separating committed funds from available funds — and applies it to student budgeting.

Every charge you log is immediately deducted from your free money, even before it clears. This means you always see what you can actually spend, not what your bank balance appears to say.

Fuzzy charges (variable amounts, known dates) hold a reservation in your budget without locking in a number — resolved to real amounts when the bill arrives.

What It Does
Built for the whole picture.
1
💰
Free Money Engine
Real-time calculation of spendable cash accounting for all committed charges. Uses Decimal arithmetic throughout — no floats, no rounding errors.
✓ Live
2
📊
Budget On-Track Signal
Three-state system (ON TRACK / CAUTION / CRISIS) with daily allowance calculation. Tells you exactly how much you can spend today.
✓ Live
3
🌫️
Fuzzy Charges
Log charges where the date is known but the amount isn't. Holds a placeholder in your budget. Resolves to a real CommittedCharge when the bill arrives.
✓ Live
4
🔄
Recurring Charges
Rent, subscriptions, tuition — log once and the next occurrence auto-generates when you mark one paid. Never manually re-enter a monthly charge.
✓ Live

Insights & Burn Rate

I built this as a CS student side project applying real software engineering — hexagonal architecture, typed domain entities, protocol-based dependency injection, and comprehensive test coverage. Every architectural decision is deliberate and documented.
