Spent — what's already gone

Every logged transaction this month. Shown as the gold segment on the timeline. 35% of the monthly limit so far in April.

Committed — bills with a fixed date and amount

Rent, gym, subscriptions. Already deducted from your Free Money the moment they're entered — so you never accidentally spend money earmarked for next week's rent.

Fuzzy — uncertain charges, handled conservatively

Course books, a planned trip. You enter a range (₪80–150). Mizān uses the midpoint in its calculation and marks it on the timeline so it's never invisible.

Safe Daily recalculates
every time you log.

Spend more today and tomorrow's allowance drops. Spend less and it grows. The math is always honest — no rounding, no forgiveness.

Calculated from your Free Money

Safe Daily = Free Money ÷ days remaining. Committed charges are already subtracted. You're never spending money that belongs to rent.

Updates on every sync

Add a transaction, hit sync, and the burn-down chart shifts immediately. The Safe Daily for tomorrow recalculates against the updated Free Money figure.

The burn-down chart shows your remaining days

Today's bar is green. Future bars are grey. As you move through the month, the chart shrinks — giving you a spatial sense of how much runway is left.

Later — next month and beyond

Bills already on your radar that don't affect this month's budget. Shown so they stay on your radar; deducted when the period arrives.

Consistency

Logging streaks keep
your numbers honest.

Every day you skip, your Free Money figure becomes an overestimate. The streak makes the gap visible before it becomes a problem.

Logging streak
12days

Your Safe Daily depends on it

Miss two days and your spent total is incomplete — the Safe Daily becomes optimistic and you overspend without knowing it. The streak is a data quality signal, not just a habit tracker.

Always visible in the sidebar

The streak box sits below the navigation, always visible. No notifications, no pressure — just a quiet nudge each time you open the dashboard.

Daily logging takes 10 seconds

Enter an amount, pick a category, done. The habit is low-friction by design — because if it costs effort, it won't happen under pressure.

Design language

Quiet. Editorial. Warm.

Mizān is designed to feel like a well-kept notebook — not a banking app. The aesthetic is calm by design, because money is stressful enough.

Arabic at the core

ميزان means "scale" or "balance" in Arabic. The wordmark uses Noto Naskh Arabic — an elegant, readable script that grounds the product in its cultural origin.
